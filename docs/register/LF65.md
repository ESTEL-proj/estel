## Feature

The coefficients read by their labels (c₁, c₂, j, ℓ, and the blocks of tensor products) from the compact buffer, in place of STL's full-grid view, `STStatistics.full_grid`. The size threshold that `full_grid` has on tensor products in 1.0.0, a temporary guard, is removed, whichever reading replaces the view

## Why

in 1.0.0 the full-grid view serves the parity with STL and the oracle, plots, and reading a coefficient by its indices. It is mostly NaN on tensor products, and reaches gigabytes there (the S4 of one map takes 3.0 GB at 128² × 32 × 32 with J = (5, 3, 3), 29 GB at 256² × 64 × 64 with J = (6, 4, 4)), which is why 1.0.0 refuses a view over 2 GiB. The parity harness is retired after 1.0.0; a labelled table is as compact as the buffer and readable

## Builds on (reserved in 1.0.0)

the compact layout and its named index tables (Part III, §19.4); `STStatistics.full_grid` (public)

## To decide

what replaces `full_grid` for reading coefficients by their labels (a labelled table, the xarray export of LF13, or both); whether `full_grid` itself is removed, which is a major version, or kept for small cases; the threshold is removed in either case

## Details

Part II, §12.7 (`full_grid`, `select`), T3.6; Part IV, §22, row of 22 November 2026, "No role for the full grid in tensor products"; issue #23. Naming the axes of the blocks is LF44. Suggested step: 2a, phase 2a.X, or with LF13

## Source

decision of the maintainer Erwan Allys on T3.2 and T3.6, 22 November 2026: the size threshold of `full_grid` on tensor products is temporary and must be removed after 1.0.0
