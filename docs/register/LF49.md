## Goal

The 2D power spectrum of a rectangular image binned by physical radial frequency, so that its bins are isotropic and estimate the same spectrum as on a square image; square images change only by the rounding of 1.0.0 removed. 2D only: no other geometry has radial bins.

## Decisions

- 25 November 2026, Pablo Richard: the exact physical frequency (question 1, answer (b), not the recommendation (a)): W_b is evaluated at each mode's physical frequency, with no radial index and no rounding. The measurement behind it: the expected binned spectrum of a k⁻³ power law with the default bins, a rectangular image against a 32 × 32 one under the same rule. Rectangle and square agree within 0.1 % under (b), against up to 8 % under (a) and up to 10 % under (c); 1.0.0 is off by ×3.1 on 32 × 64 and ×1.9 on 32 × 48. (a) adds exact rounding ties (64 modes at 32 × 64), (c) at 32 × 48. Square images change too: up to +14 % in the lowest bin and 0.5 to 4.5 % elsewhere on that spectrum, which is the rounding bias of 1.0.0 removed (the mode (1, 1), |𝐤| = 1.41, counted at 1). The card's claim that (c) leaves low radial indices with no mode was wrong: the long axis reaches every index (`docs/register/decisions.md`).
- 25 November 2026, Pablo Richard: question 2 (whether LF49 also defines the synthesis pre-filter, OP-54) is settled by LF64, which holds the pre-filter: its cut, decided on 25 November 2026, uses the same physical frequency as this binning (`docs/register/decisions.md`).
