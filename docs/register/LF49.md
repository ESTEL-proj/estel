## Goal

The 2D power spectrum of a rectangular image binned by physical radial frequency, so that its bins are isotropic and estimate the same spectrum as on a square image; square images keep their values. 2D only: no other geometry has radial bins.

## Brief

- **Question 1. The radial frequency of a mode (k₁, k₂) of an N₁ × N₂ image** (OP-6; the definition to write in `docs/maths_step2.md`).
  - (a) physical, rounded on the grid of the shorter side: index round(N_min √((k₁/N₁)² + (k₂/N₂)²)), N_min = min(N₁, N₂), in the `fft` binning and in the band maps; square images unchanged bit for bit, only the references of rectangular images change;
  - (b) the exact physical frequency, W_b evaluated at each mode with no radial index: no rounding, but square images change too;
  - (c) rounded on the grid of the longer side: a finer radial grid, with radial indices that hold no mode at low k.
  - **Recommendation:** (a): it removes the anisotropy with the smallest change of values, and keeps the range, the bins and the estimators of M-PS-2D.
- **Question 2. Scope.** The Nyquist pre-filter of the syntheses mixes mode numbers the same way on rectangular fields (M-SYN-NYQ, OP-54, "reproduce", in no card).
  - (a) LF49 also defines the pre-filter by physical frequency, (k₁/N₁)² + (k₂/N₂)² ≤ 1/4: square fields unchanged, rectangular syntheses changed (`SYNTHESIS_REFERENCE`);
  - (b) a separate card, in phase 2a.4, after W2.
  - **Recommendation:** (b): one topic per pull request (golden rule 6), and a change of syntheses needs the references of W2.

## Done when

- on 32 × 32 images the spectrum equals that of 1.0.0 bit for bit;
- on a 32 × 64 image, two modes of the same physical frequency on the two axes have the same radial index;
- on 32 × 64 fields of the process of T2.16 (64 draws), the binned spectrum agrees bin by bin with that of 32 × 32 fields within its statistical error, where 1.0.0 does not (full tier);
- `make test-spec` passes; `STATISTICS_REFERENCE` bumped with CODEOWNER approval.
