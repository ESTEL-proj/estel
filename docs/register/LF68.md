## Feature

`angular_fourier` and `scale_cosine` on tensor-product statistics, where 1.0.0 raises a clear error (OP-44), kept in step 2; `iso` on tensor products is LF26

## Why

reduced statistics of space–time data beyond `iso`; a definition block by block rests on the componentwise order of product scales, which LF27 questions: deciding both together avoids defining `scale_cosine` twice

## Builds on (reserved in 1.0.0)

reductions

## To decide

(a) block by block: `angular_fourier`, after `iso`, on the relative orientations of each averaged 2D block over [0, 2π); `scale_cosine`, the DCT of M-RED-SCALE along the first scale of each block, the index sets being Cartesian products of those of the blocks (M-IDX-TP); (b) another definition, if LF27 changes the order of product scales

## Details

maths OP-44, M-RED-ANG, M-RED-SCALE, M-IDX-TP; LF26, question 3
