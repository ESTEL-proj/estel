## Goal

The plots and diagnostics of 1.0.0 (Part II §12.10) on every geometry and reduction that 1.0.0 has or that the cards of step 2a bring: the spectra of 1D and tensor-product data (LF10) and `iso` on tensor products (LF26). A `gaussianise` that draws a Gaussian field with the target's mean and power spectrum, its generator chosen after a comparison. The needs of agents collected on the issue #186. The extensions that wait for later cards, HEALPix spectrum plots, the Fourier diagnostics on HEALPix and the plots of the tensor-product reductions of LF68, are LF74.

## Brief

**The comparison of the generators of `gaussianise`** (a draft for the maintainer's review; not run). What it settles is the field `decide`.
- **Candidates.** (i) Phase randomisation: the target's Fourier moduli kept, random phases: its periodogram exactly, but the moduli do not vary as those of a Gaussian field do. (ii) White noise convolved with the target's own spectrum, its periodogram: that periodogram in expectation, with varying moduli (Pablo Richard's suggestion). (iii) The same with the binned spectrum as kernel, the smooth curve that the statistics hold: LF59's start generator (its decisions of 25 November 2026). (iv) IAAFT: not Gaussian; it keeps the distribution of the values exactly and the spectrum approximately. Each with the target's mean.
- **Criterion 1, on Gaussian fields of known spectrum.** For one target realisation, the draws of each candidate against independent realisations of the same process: per coefficient of S1 to S4 and of the binned spectrum, the offset of the mean of the draws from the mean of the realisations, in units of the realisations' standard deviation, and the ratio of the spreads. A good generator gives draws that the statistics cannot tell from independent realisations. Expected: (i) a spread too small on S2 and the spectrum; (ii) the right spread about the target's own sample, whose noise it copies; (iii) the right spread about the binned spectrum, with the smoothing of the bins as its bias; (iv) close to (i).
- **Criterion 2, on non-Gaussian fields** (the log-normal fields of `tests/consistency/` and of the benchmark): what each candidate keeps (the mean, the variance, the binned and the unbinned spectrum, the histogram of the values) and how far S3 and S4 move from the target's towards the values of a Gaussian field: `gaussianise` is the Gaussian reference against which the statistics show what is not Gaussian.
- **Criterion 3, reach and cost.** Which candidates extend to non-periodic and masked data, to 1D and tensor products, to HEALPix (spherical harmonics, LF24); the cost of a draw (one FFT pair for (i) to (iii), the iterations of IAAFT).
- **The smallest data that shows it.** Periodic 2D, 32 × 32, J = 3, L = 4 (6 bins of the spectrum), float64; 8 targets, 64 draws of each candidate per target and 64 independent realisations of the process; 64 × 64 only if 32 × 32 does not separate the candidates. Outside CI, the results on #186.
- **Decided afterwards:** the default generator; one function with `method=` or several functions; their names, which must not clash with "surrogates" (the overview's word for syntheses, produced by `synthesize_from_maps`, which in 1.0.0 takes periodic 2D data and full HEALPix skies without missing values, LF47); whether IAAFT, which is not Gaussian, belongs here at all; then its definition in `docs/maths_step2.md`.
