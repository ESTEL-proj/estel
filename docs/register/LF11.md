## Goal

Plots and diagnostics for every geometry and reduction; once defined, a `gaussianise` transform and surrogate fields, common needs of users and agents (Part II §12.10).

## Brief

The entry holds four deliverables; the specification defines neither `gaussianise` nor surrogates.
**Question 1. What is "improved plotting and diagnostics"?**
(a) The utilities of 1.0.0 (§12.10) extended to every geometry and reduction they do not cover yet: cheap, no new mathematics.
(b) A list of needs collected from users and agents first: scope and cost unknown.
**Recommendation:** (a); other improvements come as ordinary issues, since "improved" alone cannot brief an agent.
**Question 2. What does `gaussianise` compute?**
(a) Marginal Gaussianisation by ranks: each map's values replaced by the Gaussian quantiles of their ranks, per channel, missing pixels kept, with its inverse; in NumPy, cheap, not differentiable.
(b) A differentiable parametric transform (Box–Cox or Yeo–Johnson), usable inside a loss: a fit per map, more mathematics.
(c) Dropped.
**Recommendation:** (a), the usual meaning and the cheapest.
**Question 3. What is a surrogate?**
(a) A phase-randomised field: the power spectrum of the data, random phases, Gaussian; the reference against which the statistics show what is not Gaussian; cheap with the FFT, periodic data.
(b) Syntheses from the statistics of the data: already `synthesize_from_maps` with `n_batch` (ensembles: LF12).
**Recommendation:** (a), since (b) exists; seeded, as every random draw of ESTEL.

## Tasks

1. Cheap: the plots of §12.10 for 1D, tensor-product, HEALPix and reduced statistics.
2. Cheap: the diagnostics of §12.10 on every geometry they do not cover yet.
3. [human] Per questions 2 and 3: the definitions in `docs/maths_step2.md`, with `M-` IDs; the names in `api/manifest.json`.
4. `gaussianise`, then the surrogates, each with its oracle and tests.

## Done when

Each plot returns a matplotlib `Figure` for every geometry, never calls `plt.show()` and imports no backend (§12.10); each diagnostic runs on every geometry; `gaussianise` gives Gaussian marginals and its inverse returns the input to float precision; a surrogate has the power spectrum of its data within the float64 tolerance and changes with its seed.
