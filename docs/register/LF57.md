## Goal

Non-periodic means keep as many pixels as their bias allows, and no coarse coefficient is computed silently on a reduced border (OP-13, OP-61): planar 2D and 1D, and the non-periodic blocks of tensor products (HEALPix has no border, M-MASK-HPX).

## Decisions

- 23 November 2026, Pablo Richard: a mean whose crop leaves too few pixels is not computed: it is NaN, with one warning, as an empty mean (M-MEAN, OP-3), so that no value is biased silently (question 2, answer (b)); the study of question 1 has started (`docs/register/decisions.md`).
- 24 November 2026, Pablo Richard: the crop rule is (b) with ε = 0.05 (question 1, from the study, issue #158). A pixel is cropped where the padded values carry more than 5 % of its weight, through every operation of the cascade and the wavelet; the plan computes the widths from the kernels. It closes OP-61's gap at 64² and keeps every coefficient of 128² with J = 5; at 64² the level j = 4 keeps no pixel, so its coefficients are NaN (question 2). With STL's kernels, M-WIN's common window becomes 3 px. Any ε in [0.04, 0.07] works on the study's process, STL's kernels, J = 5 and L = 4: to measure again when LF22 changes the kernels (`docs/register/decisions.md`).
