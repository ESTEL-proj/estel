## Goal

Non-periodic means keep as many pixels as their bias allows, and no coarse coefficient is computed silently on a reduced border (OP-13, OP-61): planar 2D and 1D, and the non-periodic blocks of tensor products (HEALPix has no border, M-MASK-HPX).

## Decisions

- 23 November 2026, Pablo Richard: a mean whose crop leaves too few pixels is not computed: it is NaN, with one warning, as an empty mean (M-MEAN, OP-3), so that no value is biased silently (question 2, answer (b)); the study of question 1 has started (`docs/register/decisions.md`).

## Brief

**Question 1. The crop rule, an M- entry to write in `docs/maths_step2.md` (none yet).**
- (a) M-BORDER's count by the kernels' support (1.0.0): 2 pixels per convolution at its level; LF33's common window (M-WIN) takes 4 at every level, so the coarsest level of 128² with J = 5 (8 pixels) no longer holds it, where S1 and S2 now keep 4 × 4 pixels with no gap (OP-61).
- (b) The reach of the padding weighted by the kernels: crop a pixel where the padded values carry more than a fraction ε of its weight, through every operation (cascade, wavelet, low-pass: LF60); one ε for every family, the widths computed from the kernels by the plan.
- (c) A width per family and level, tuned on measured bias: fits STL's kernels only, to tune again when LF22 changes them.
- **Recommendation:** the study below, then (b) if one ε closes the gap of OP-61 at 64² and keeps the coarse means of 128²; it also tells whether the common window of M-WIN can stay. Status `study`.

**Study** (settles question 1). Extend `tests/consistency/` (T2.16) with crop widths 0 to 4 pixels per side and rule (b) for a few ε, with per-pair and common windows: 64² and 128², J = 5, L = 4, 512 and 256 draws; report the smallest crop with no gap and the pixels it keeps. About a day.
