## Goal

`CrossSpectrumOperator` and `compute_PS` work on 1D and tensor-product data, with binned spectra, joint and low-pass bins, through the reserved `method=` names (Part II §9.4).

## Brief

**Question 1. Should the 1D and tensor-product spectra ship directly with the generalised v1.6.0 estimators (LF10), or first as in STL's `2D+1D`, then generalised?**
(a) Directly, LF23 and LF10 as one piece of work: the bins of `2D+1D` with the four v1.6.0 estimators on the axes of each block (§20.5); one maths entry, one implementation; no reference bump, since ESTEL has no such spectrum before.
(b) First as in `2D+1D`, against parity fixtures from STL, then LF10: STL's numbers checked, but two implementations, two maths entries, a reference bump, and parity tests that LF10 removes.
**Recommendation:** (a), because the decisions of 20 November 2026 keep no STL behaviour, so (b) ships a spectrum that the next release drops; on periodic data without NaN, where the `fft` estimator should agree with `2D+1D`, compare once in the pull request, without a kept parity test.
