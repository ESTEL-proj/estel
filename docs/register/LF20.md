## Goal

One canonical set of coefficients in every geometry (planar 2D and 1D, tensor products, HEALPix), the low-pass terms of LF6 included: what is computed, stored, returned by `to_vector` and weighted in the synthesis loss, while `full_grid` still gives the tensors of 1.0.0.

## Decisions

- 24 November 2026, Pablo Richard: the non-redundant set is canonical (question 1, answer (b)). At j₁ = j₂, S4 keeps ℓ₁ ≤ ℓ₂ for an auto term and one channel order for a cross pair; 1D with L = 2 and products of 1D blocks only keep one of two conjugate orientations. The losses change (`docs/register/decisions.md`).
- 24 November 2026, Pablo Richard: `to_vector` returns the canonical set (question 2, answer (a)): one real for an entry real by construction (auto S1, S2 and PS; the diagonal of S4, c₁ = c₂, j₁ = j₂, ℓ₁ = ℓ₂; every term whose last filter is low-pass in every block, Φ being real), two for any other, as the `is_complex` of a segment. Its squared norm is the complex loss of M-SYN-LOSS on the canonical set: `to_vector` and the loss become one vector (OP-39, with LF53) (`docs/register/decisions.md`).
