## Goal

Gradients of the Python engine, as in a synthesis, keep about a third of the maps they keep today (M-GRAD), second order included; or the entry closes with its measurement recorded.

## Brief

**Question 1. Should the Python engine recompute the second layer per level and band orientation during the backward pass (M-GRAD)?**
(a) By default: the maps kept fall from about 75 to about 25 reals per pixel (M-GRAD), for a second forward pass of the second layer; on CPU at 512 × 512, after the Gram form, T2.14 measured no lower memory and a gradient 12 to 53 % slower.
(b) As an option, for large maps or a tight device memory: a choice that depends on the use, allowed after 1.0.0 (Part IV, §22, "Global optimisations only in 1.0.0"); the default stays as today.
(c) Not at all: the card closes, its result recorded in Part III, §19.14.
**Recommendation:** status `study`: measure in the optimisation session of the benchmark of step 2 (LF62), on GPU, where the allocated device memory counts the arrays exactly (T2.11 measured the peak resident memory of the process), and on its larger case; then (b) if the peak with gradients falls by at least a third, else (c).
