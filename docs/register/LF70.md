## Feature

L-BFGS optimises v with u = A v, where A multiplies in Fourier space by √PS_target(k) inside the Nyquist disk and by 0 beyond it. A white start in v is then LF59's spectral start, the power beyond the disk is zero by construction (it replaces the projection), and L-BFGS sees a flat spectrum, so red spectra no longer let the large scales dominate the gradients. It costs one FFT pair per evaluation

## Why

the speed and quality of syntheses, measured on the benchmark of step 2 (LF62)
