## Feature

The plots and diagnostics of LF11 extended to what later cards bring: HEALPix spectrum plots and the Fourier diagnostics on HEALPix (`frequency_response`, `littlewood_paley`, `energy_partition`, `aliasing`), which need spherical harmonics (LF24); the plots of the tensor-product reductions moved to LF68

## Why

LF11 covers every geometry and reduction that 1.0.0 and the cards of step 2a bring; these extensions need HEALPix spectra (LF24, step 2b) and the reductions of LF68 (step 3), so they cannot be done with it

## Builds on (reserved in 1.0.0)

the utilities of Part II §12.10 (T6.2), as LF11 extends them

## To decide

the Fourier diagnostics on the sphere, defined through spherical harmonics; the plots of `angular_fourier` and `scale_cosine` on tensor products, as LF68 defines them

## Details

LF11, LF24, LF68; Part II §12.10. Suggested step: 3, after LF24 (step 2b) and LF68 (step 3)
