## Feature

A J\_min for the scattering statistics: the finest scales, j < J\_min, left out. Either one `Jmin` shared by the scattering statistics and the spectrum, or at least a J\_min implemented for the scattering statistics if the spectral coverage of the spectrum is no longer tied to J

## Why

One J, set on `STOperator`, governs both the scales of the scattering statistics and the band of frequencies of the spectrum: with J\_min = 0 the spectrum spans the centres of the scales 0 to J − 1 (maths §1.9, "Range"; M-WAV-2D, ξ₀). `Jmin`, as in STL, cuts the spectrum only: with `Jmin` > 0 the two bands no longer match. One name for one object

## Builds on (reserved in 1.0.0)

`Jmin` of `STOperator`, today an option of the spectrum only (Part II, §12.6; maths §1.9, "Options")

## To decide

whether `Jmin` is shared by the scattering statistics and the spectrum, or implemented for the scattering statistics alone if the spectral coverage of the spectrum is no longer tied to J (LF10, LF61)

## Details

STL's scattering loop always starts at the finest scale, j = 0; a J\_min of the scattering statistics is a capability STL lacks, so it comes after 1.0.0 (AGENTS.md, golden rule 11). Erwan's words: "min commun aux ST et au spectre, ou en tout cas implémenté pour les ST si la couverture spectrale du spectre n'est plus reliée à J". Suggested step: with LF10 (2a.X)
