## Feature

The power spectrum binned up to the corners of the frequency square, and wavelets whose supports cover the corners, with more sample variance there

## Why

today every statistic but the variance is blind beyond the Nyquist disk, so step 2 restricts the synthesis model to the disk (LF59, "The Nyquist disk"); statistics that cover the corners would let the model use every scale, with non-Gaussian constraints there

## Builds on (reserved in 1.0.0)

the spectrum estimators; `WaveletOperator`

## To decide

the radial bins and the wavelets that cover the corners, and the sample variance they accept; whether the scale space of the synthesis model then extends beyond the disk (LF59)

## Details

LF59, "The Nyquist disk"; LF64; maths OP-32, M-PS-2D ("Frequencies and bins"), M-SYN-NYQ
