## Goal

`iso`, `angular_fourier` and `scale_cosine` on tensor-product statistics (space–time 2D+1D, 2D+1D+1D), where 1.0.0 raises a clear error (OP-44): an isotropy in space that keeps the direction of propagation.

## Brief

**Question 1. Over what does `iso` average on a product (the definition to write)?**
- (a) the rotations of the 2D block over [0, 2π): a rotation by π maps a product orientation to the conjugate of the one whose 1D orientations are swapped (ψ₁ = conj ψ₀); the 1D blocks are not averaged, so the direction of propagation relative to the arms is kept. M-RED-ISO on the 2D axis alone would not be invariant, since a rotation by π also swaps the 1D orientations.
- (b) the rotations and time reversal: the real parts only; the direction of propagation is lost.
- **Recommendation:** (a), LF32's rule (rotations of the 2D blocks, nothing on a line) applied to products; on a product of one 2D block it gives LF32's real parts.

**Question 2. Which products?** The orientations stored for products of 1D blocks only are redundant by 2, and those of products with two 2D blocks or more cover half of the distinct product filters (ψₐ ⊗ conj ψ_b is missing).
- (a) one 2D block with 1D blocks: question 1; 1D blocks only: the canonical statistics unchanged, as in 1D (LF32, question 2); several 2D blocks: a clear error, before the computation.
- (b) a clear error for every product other than one 2D block with 1D blocks.
- **Recommendation:** (a), because it defines `iso` wherever the stored orientations allow it, and like 1D for products of 1D blocks.

**Question 3. `angular_fourier` and `scale_cosine` on products?**
- (a) block by block: `angular_fourier`, after `iso`, on the relative orientations of the 2D block over [0, 2π); `scale_cosine`, the DCT of M-RED-SCALE along the first scale of each block, the index sets being Cartesian products of those of the blocks (M-IDX-TP).
- (b) `iso` only; the other two keep the clear error of OP-44.
- **Recommendation:** (a), because the transforms separate over the blocks, and the entry names all three.
