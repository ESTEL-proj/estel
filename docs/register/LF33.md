## Feature

Centred covariances: S2, S3 and S4 computed as covariances, by default, wherever the plan cannot prove that one argument has a zero mean; `var` centred too (OP-11)

## Why

the exact definition; indispensable as soon as a low-pass filter is the last filter (LF6); corrects border and mask effects

## Builds on (reserved in 1.0.0)

the `centring` field of the plan, first moments in the reference

## To decide

how the means of the maps that end with a low-pass filter are centred, coefficients corrected or maps centred first: the most efficient way that keeps the precision in single precision (§20.11, rule 1; maths_step2 M-COV)
