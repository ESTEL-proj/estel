## Goal

`var`, S2, S3 and S4 become covariances wherever the plan cannot prove a zero mean (non-periodic borders, masks, partial skies, low-pass terms), in planar 2D and 1D, tensor products and HEALPix; on periodic data without mask, S1 to S4 keep their values and their cost.

## Decisions

- 23 November 2026, Pablo Richard: the centring in single precision is mixed (question 1, answer (d)). The band-pass maps are corrected on their coefficients, their mean being small (OP-36); the maps that end with a low-pass filter are centred first, with a mean computed in float64, then corrected for their small residual mean, which keeps the centring exact on whatever windows LF57 sets (`docs/register/decisions.md`).
- 23 November 2026, Pablo Richard: `center` is not an option. Every covariance is centred on its own means, wherever the plan cannot prove a zero mean, and gradients flow through the means. STL's raw moments (`none`) are not kept (decision "Versions" of 20 November 2026); centring on the means of a reference (`ref`) waits for a use (`docs/register/decisions.md`).
- 23 November 2026, Pablo Richard: the changes of values. In 1.0.0 `var` is the raw second moment (M-MOM), so it changes whenever the mean is not zero; on periodic data without mask and without low-pass terms, S1 to S4 are unchanged (`docs/register/decisions.md`).
