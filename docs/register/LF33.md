## Goal

`var`, S2, S3 and S4 become covariances wherever the plan cannot prove a zero mean (non-periodic borders, masks, partial skies, low-pass terms), in planar 2D and 1D, tensor products and HEALPix; on periodic data without mask, S1 to S4 keep their values and their cost.

## Brief

**Question 1. How are the maps whose mean dominates centred, in float32?**
- (a) Coefficients corrected, C = G − m m^H, sums in the data precision: the simplest; error up to 2e-4 of the covariance on low-pass maps (white noise 1024², M-COV table).
- (b) Coefficients corrected, G and m summed in float64: 4e-8 to 9e-8; the Gram products in float64, slow where the device's float64 rate is low [to verify, LF62].
- (c) Every map centred first, with a float64 mean: 4e-8 to 9e-8; a centred copy of every map (no in-place update), exact only on one window per level (M-WIN).
- (d) Mixed, as M-COV sketches: band-pass maps corrected on the coefficients (their mean is small, OP-36); maps ending with a low-pass filter centred first with a float64 mean, then corrected for their small residual mean.
- **Recommendation:** (d), because it has the precision of (b) and (c) at the cost of (a): only the maps ending with a low-pass filter have a dominant mean, and they live at the coarsest level; the residual correction keeps it exact on whatever windows LF57 sets.

- Periodic data without mask and without low-pass term: S1 to S4 unchanged within `tests/tolerances.toml`, time unchanged on the benchmark; `var` is the variance, `mean` stored apart (rule 7).
- x → x + c leaves every statistic but `mean` unchanged in every geometry, masks included: `test_constant_shift` extended to all its cases.
- In float32, on the cases of the M-COV table (full tier), the error against float64 stays within the float32 tolerance (M-COV measures 4e-8 to 9e-8).
- Second-order gradients flow through m with `self` on PyTorch and JAX; with `ref`, μ is a constant.
- `tests/consistency/` passes; the removed parity tests are listed in the PR.

## Feature

Centred covariances: S2, S3 and S4 computed as covariances, by default, wherever the plan cannot prove that one argument has a zero mean; `var` centred too (OP-11)

## Why
