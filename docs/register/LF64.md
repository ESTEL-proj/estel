## Decisions

- 25 November 2026, Pablo Richard: the cut is the exact physical one, (k₁/N₁)² + (k₂/N₂)² ≤ 1/4, computed in integers as 4(k₁²N₂² + k₂²N₁²) ≤ N₁²N₂²: a disk in physical frequency, an ellipse in mode numbers, at ½ cycle per pixel on both axes, where the coverage of the wavelets ends, since the same kernel serves both axes. Square fields are unchanged bit for bit. A circle in mode numbers mixes different spatial scales on the two axes, which is not what is wanted: the power spectrum must be binned on the same ellipses, not on circular rings (LF49, OP-6), so LF64 and LF49 use the same physical frequency, and LF64 depends on LF49 (`docs/register/decisions.md`).

## Feature

The Nyquist pre-filter of syntheses cuts at the same spatial frequency along both axes of a rectangular field

## Why

the pre-filter takes the radius of the shorter side in mode numbers on both axes, so on the longer axis the cut is at a lower spatial frequency (OP-54); the same flaw as the radial binning of the 2D spectrum (LF49, OP-6)
