## Goal

The cross-channel S1 regularised without an absolute constant, in every geometry (planar 2D and 1D, tensor products, HEALPix, and the low-pass scales of LF6): multiplying a channel by a > 0 multiplies its cross S1 by √a exactly, and leaves the normalised cross S1 unchanged.

## Brief

**Question 1. What replaces ε = 10⁻⁸ in M-S1 (the definition to write)?**
- (a) no ε: z / |z|^½ with the safe modulus; exactly covariant, but its derivative grows as |z|^−½ near a zero of z, and its second derivative as |z|^−3/2: a risk in float32 and for the second order that the engine must give.
- (b) an ε relative to each channel, scale and orientation: ε_c(j, ℓ) = η ⟨|W_{j,ℓ} x_c|⟩, the mean modulus of the same map with its mask and border, computed even where the auto S1 is not stored (OP-30), with η = 10⁻⁸ and the safe division where z and ε vanish together (a null channel); covariant, bounded derivatives; standardised data move by about 10⁻⁸ relative.
- (c) an ε from the normalisation reference σ_c(j, ℓ): undefined with `norm="none"` and constant with `norm="ref"`, so not covariant.
- **Recommendation:** (b), because it is the only option both covariant and bounded in its derivatives, for one mean per map whose modulus is already computed.
