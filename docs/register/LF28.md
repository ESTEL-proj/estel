## Goal

The cross-channel S1 regularised without an absolute constant, in every geometry (planar 2D and 1D, tensor products, HEALPix, and the low-pass scales of LF6): multiplying a channel by a > 0 multiplies its cross S1 by √a exactly, and leaves the normalised cross S1 unchanged.

## Decisions

- 24 November 2026, Pablo Richard: an ε relative to each channel, scale and orientation replaces ε = 10⁻⁸ in M-S1 (question 1, answer (b)): ε_c(j, ℓ) = η ⟨|W_{j,ℓ} x_c|⟩, the mean modulus of the same map with its mask and border, computed even where the auto S1 is not stored (OP-30), with η = 10⁻⁸ and the safe division where z and ε vanish together (a null channel). It is covariant, with bounded derivatives (`docs/register/decisions.md`).
