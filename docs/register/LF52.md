## Goal

With missing data, each coarse pixel enters the means with the weight of its observed fraction: no threshold (0.33 in 2D and on HEALPix, 0.5 in 1D), and no pixel decided by the last bit of the kernel in float32; for planar 2D and 1D and for HEALPix, and for tensor products when their masks come (LF8, step 2b).

## Brief

- **Question 1. The rule of a downsampled pixel** (M-DOWN-MASK, OP-12; the definition to write in `docs/maths_step2.md`).
  - (a) 1.0.0: value × 1/(1 − f), invalid if f > τ, weight 0 or 1;
  - (b) normalised convolution: value M-DOWN(w x) / M-DOWN(w), weight M-DOWN(w) clipped to [0, 1] (the planar kernels have negative taps), the divisor floored (10⁻⁸, as on HEALPix); at the first step w is the 0/1 mask and the value is that of 1.0.0; every mean weighted (M-MEAN; the "weights" windows of M-WIN);
  - (c) one τ for every geometry, nothing else: removes only the difference between 2D and 1D.
  - **Recommendation:** (b) if the study of question 3 confirms it, else (c).
- **Question 2. The weight of a convolved pixel** (M-MASK: today 0 when the wavelet support E touches an invalid pixel); both options give 1.0.0 at level 0.
  - (a) 0 if E touches a pixel of weight 0, else the pixel's own weight;
  - (b) the smallest weight over E.
  - **Recommendation:** (b): a value computed from an amplified neighbour (f near 1) is weighted down.
- **Question 3. Does the consistency test of T2.16 settle it?** Not as it stands: 1.0.0's masked estimate already passes it (largest masked |z| 3.75; no gap recorded, OP-61 is on borders), so a pass or a fail cannot rank (a) and (b). It does with two additions, a study of at most a day in `tests/consistency/`: for each variant, the RMS over the draws of the paired difference masked − periodic, per family and scale (bias and variance together); and masks with many partly valid coarse pixels (strips 1 to 3 pixels wide, 20 % of scattered pixels) besides the five holes; N = 128 and 256, 64 draws, J = 5, 2D and 1D.
  - **Recommendation:** status `study`; (b) if its RMS is nowhere larger than (a)'s and no |z| exceeds 6.1, with the rule of question 2 that the study prefers.

## Feature

Masks downsampled as continuous weights: each coarse pixel keeps its valid fraction as its weight in the means, instead of the threshold τ and the reweighting 1/(1−f) (a proposal, to examine)

## Why

no arbitrary thresholds (0.33 in 2D, 0.5 in 1D), and no pixel decided by the last bit of the kernel, which may differ in float32 (OP-12)
