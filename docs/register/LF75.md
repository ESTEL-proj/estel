## Feature

A real name for the wavelet of HEALPix data: today it is `"healpix"`, the name of the grid, not of the wavelet (STL's `"HealpixWavelet"` has the same flaw)

## Why

the names of ESTEL follow the tree of geometries (Part I, §2.4.1); a wavelet named after its grid says nothing of the wavelet, and leaves no name for another wavelet on the same grid

## Builds on (reserved in 1.0.0)

the `wavelet` argument of the wavelet operator (Part II, §12.5)

## To decide

the name, from the definition of the HEALPix kernel (maths M-WAV-HPX)

## Details

left out of phase P7 because the name enters the key of a plan held in memory (`wavelets/_operator_healpix.py`); it is not written in saved files, whose `"geometry": "healpix"` is the tag of the data. Suggested step: 2a.X
