## Goal

`iso` and `angular_fourier` consistent with the conjugation of the orientations across π, in planar 2D and on HEALPix (whose gauges also cover [0, π)), defined in 1D whatever L (OP-40), the low-pass terms of LF6 included; LF26 extends `iso` to tensor products, and LF68 (step 3) the transforms.

## Decisions

- 25 November 2026, Pablo Richard: `iso` returns only the real parts of the reduced S3 and S4 and of the reduced cross S1 and S2 (question 1, answer (a)): the average over the 2L band orientations of [0, 2π), invariant under rotations by π/L; the reduced S3 and S4 halve (`docs/register/decisions.md`).
- 25 November 2026, Pablo Richard: in 1D with L = 2, `iso` gives the statistics of L = 1 (question 2, answer (b)): on real data the entries of ℓ = 1 are conjugates or copies of those of ℓ = 0, which LF20 drops; L = 1 is unchanged. An operator built on 1D data with L = 2, never on the 1D blocks of a tensor product, emits a UserWarning: "L = 2 on real 1D data gives the statistics of L = 1 (ℓ = 1 is the conjugate filter); use L = 1. L = 2 is for the 1D blocks of tensor products." With LF20 only ℓ = 0 is computed, so the warning is about redundancy, not cost (`docs/register/decisions.md`).
- 25 November 2026, Pablo Richard: without `iso`, `angular_fourier` transforms the band orientation over the 2L orientations of [0, 2π), the second half filled by conjugation (question 3, answer (b), not the recommendation (a)): exact; even harmonics carry the real part, odd harmonics the imaginary part; a rotation by π/L multiplies each harmonic by a phase; LF20's canonical set stores only the non-redundant half (`docs/register/decisions.md`).
