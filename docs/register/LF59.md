## Goal

A synthesis starts from a field with the target's moments, mean 0 and variance 1 in its standardised units, instead of a variance near π/4 (0.78 at 32 × 32), so that its first iterations no longer go to the variance; for every geometry with syntheses (periodic planar data in 1.0.0, T6.1).

## Decisions

- 25 November 2026, Pablo Richard: the standardisation is over the running batch (question 2, answer (b), not the recommendation (a)): the batch of starts together has mean 0 and variance 1, chosen for the mean-field approach; the starts keep their sample fluctuations about the batch (`docs/register/decisions.md`).
- 25 November 2026, Pablo Richard: the start is standardised (question 1, answer (a)): a given `init` and a checkpoint are taken as they are, and `mean` and `var` stay in the loss; together with the rules of "The Nyquist disk" (`docs/register/decisions.md`):
  1. The target is filtered to the disk before any statistic that sees beyond it, its variance first. From maps this already holds (M-SYN-TARGET). From statistics, the statistics record whether their maps were pre-filtered, through a pre-filter option of the statistics API (an API addition, through `api/manifest.json` first), and a synthesis from statistics computed without the pre-filter raises a warning that says to recompute them with it.
  2. The running field stays in the disk at every evaluation: the loss is computed on P(v), with P the pre-filter (a projection), at the cost of one FFT pair per evaluation. How much power the gradients of 1.0.0 leak beyond the disk, through the 5 × 5 wavelets and the smoothing kernel cropped in pixel space, is to be measured (task 4).
  3. The start is filtered first, then the batch of starts is centred and scaled together to mean 0 and variance 1 (question 2, answer (b)).
- 25 November 2026, Pablo Richard: the Nyquist filtering may also be coded as a kernel filtering option, a convolution in pixel space, besides the cut in Fourier space; the M- entry states both. A kernel is not an exact projection: with it, "no power beyond the disk" holds only approximately (`docs/register/decisions.md`).

## The Nyquist disk

Pablo Richard's rationale for this card, documented here at his request on 25 November 2026 (`docs/register/decisions.md`).

Except in 1D, every statistic of the loss but the variance is blind beyond the Nyquist disk. The wavelets do not cover the corners of the frequency square (OP-32), the radial bins of the spectrum stop at N_max, and `mean` is the zero mode. The variance alone sees every scale. This is not imposed by the mathematics: a spectrum could bin up to the corners, and wavelets could cover them, with more sample variance there.

If the degrees of freedom of the model are all the scales, the scales beyond the disk are constrained by the variance alone. The synthesis, which mimics a maximum-entropy model, then leaves them close to Gaussian, and they can bias statistics that the loss does not hold, the PDF for instance.

The chosen strategy is therefore to restrict the degrees of freedom of the model to the Nyquist disk. The running field has no power beyond the disk at any time of the synthesis, and the target is filtered to the disk (M-SYN-NYQ) before any statistic that depends on scales beyond it is estimated, its variance first. On rectangular fields, the disk is the physical disk of LF64, an ellipse in mode numbers.

The maths and code choices that follow from this card are documented: its `M-` entry, and the docstrings of the synthesis functions (task 3).

## Done when

- the batch of starts, filtered to the disk, together has mean 0 and variance 1 to 1e-12 (float64; 16 × 16 and 32 × 32, `nbatch` 1 and 2); a given `init` and a resumed checkpoint are unchanged;
- with the cut in Fourier space, the field on which the loss is computed has no power beyond the disk at any evaluation, to rounding;
- statistics computed with the pre-filter record it, and a synthesis from statistics computed without it raises the warning that says to recompute them with it;
- the leak of task 4 is measured and reported;
- `make test-spec` passes; `SYNTHESIS_REFERENCE` bumped with CODEOWNER approval.
