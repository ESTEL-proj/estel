## Goal

A synthesis starts from a field with the target's moments, mean 0 and variance 1 in its standardised units, instead of a variance near π/4 (0.78 at 32 × 32), so that its first iterations no longer go to the variance; for every geometry with syntheses (periodic planar data in 1.0.0, T6.1).

## Decisions

- 25 November 2026, Pablo Richard: the standardisation is over the running batch (question 2, answer (b), not the recommendation (a)): the batch of starts together has mean 0 and variance 1, chosen for the mean-field approach; the starts keep their sample fluctuations about the batch (`docs/register/decisions.md`).

## The Nyquist disk

Pablo Richard's rationale for this card, documented here at his request on 25 November 2026 (`docs/register/decisions.md`).

Except in 1D, every statistic of the loss but the variance is blind beyond the Nyquist disk. The wavelets do not cover the corners of the frequency square (OP-32), the radial bins of the spectrum stop at N_max, and `mean` is the zero mode. The variance alone sees every scale. This is not imposed by the mathematics: a spectrum could bin up to the corners, and wavelets could cover them, with more sample variance there.

If the degrees of freedom of the model are all the scales, the scales beyond the disk are constrained by the variance alone. The synthesis, which mimics a maximum-entropy model, then leaves them close to Gaussian, and they can bias statistics that the loss does not hold, the PDF for instance.

The chosen strategy is therefore to restrict the degrees of freedom of the model to the Nyquist disk. The running field has no power beyond the disk at any time of the synthesis, and the target is filtered to the disk (M-SYN-NYQ) before any statistic that depends on scales beyond it is estimated, its variance first. On rectangular fields, the disk is the physical disk of LF64, an ellipse in mode numbers.

The maths and code choices that follow from this card are documented: its `M-` entry, and the docstrings of the synthesis functions (task 2).

## Brief

- **Question 1. What is standardised** (OP-73; the definition to write in `docs/maths_step2.md`).
  - (a) the start only: the Gaussian start, after the pre-filter (M-SYN-NYQ), centred and scaled to variance 1; a given `init` and a checkpoint are taken as they are; `mean` and `var` stay in the loss;
  - (b) the running field at every evaluation: Φ computed on (u − ⟨u⟩)/σ(u), so that `mean` and `var` match by construction and leave the loss, which no longer depends on the scale of u;
  - (c) both.
  - **Recommendation:** (a): its answer is known, the target's moments; (b) changes the problem the optimiser solves, whose merit is the quality of the syntheses, which the decisions of 20 November 2026 place in step 2b.
