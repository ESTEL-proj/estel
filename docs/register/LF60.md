The low-pass terms of non-periodic data (the tensor products of 1.0.0, then every planar geometry with LF6) are averaged over pixels the replicated padding does not bias, or the zero border is shown harmless; planar 2D and 1D and tensor products (HEALPix has no border, M-MASK-HPX).

## Brief

**Question 1. Does the zero border at a block's low-pass scale hold (OP-81)?**
- (a) Keep β = 0 at the low-pass scale (M-BORDER-TP) and for LF6's maps: no pixel lost; yet the padding reaches 2 pixels per side at every level from 2 on through the cascade, and 4 before and 5 after on Φ x at level J − 1 (counted from M-DOWN's taps and padding).
- (b) Count the cascade and the last smoothing by their support, like convolutions: exact, but no pixel left on a level of 9 pixels or fewer, such as the low-pass terms of 128² with J = 5.
- (c) Count them by the weight the padded values carry (rule (b) of LF57): crops only where a bias could show.
- **Recommendation:** keep the status `study`; then (a) if no gap shows, otherwise (c).

**Study.** Extend `tests/consistency/` (T2.16: log-normal field, the same field with `pbc=False` and `pbc=True`, paired z, threshold 6.1) to the low-pass terms, centred: the tensor-product low-pass and mixed scales of 1.0.0 (M-LP), and LF6's terms in 2D and 1D from the `_spec` of LF6 and LF33; crops (a), (b) and (c); 64² and 128² with J = 5, L = 4, and 1D of the same lengths, 256 to 512 draws; report each coefficient's z and the pixels each crop keeps. About a day.
