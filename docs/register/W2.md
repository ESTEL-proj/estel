## Goal

Syntheses get reference outputs per backend, versioned by `SYNTHESIS_REFERENCE`, so that every later change of a synthesis (LF58, LF59, then LF56, LF12) is seen and approved; for every geometry with syntheses (periodic 2D data in 1.0.0, T6.1).

## Inputs

- Read: Part I §7.1–§7.2 (`SYNTHESIS_REFERENCE`), §5.1 (the row "Synthesis"), §5.2 (the calibration of tolerances), §1.6 (pinned dependencies); Part II §15.2, T6.1 (the comparison with STL and its tolerance); maths M-SYN-LBFGS, M-SYN-OUT, §5.8 (checkpoints); `tests/reference/AGENTS.md`.
- Modules: `src/estel/synthesis/` (from T6.1); `tests/reference/`, `tests/tolerances.toml`, `src/estel/_reference.py` and `tools/make_reference.py` (protected); `tools/version_impact.py` (`COUNTERS`: the directory of the synthesis references); the comparison of T6.1 in `tests/parity/`.

## Tasks

1. The reference cases and their form: for each, the loss history over the first iterations and the field after them, from PyTorch on CPU in float64, with the versions of the dependencies (Part I §1.6); from maps and from statistics, `mean_field` True and False, Nc 1 and 2, `nbatch` 1 and 2, 32 × 32, J = 3 (the cases of M-SYN-TARGET, "Checked"), for each geometry with syntheses; their tests, with tolerances calibrated as in Part I §5.2.
2. [human] The first references generated by a maintainer; `SYNTHESIS_REFERENCE` from 0 to 1; `tools/version_impact.py` given their directory.
3. The comparison with STL of T6.1 extended to the cases of task 1, in the full tier, before LF58 lands (LF58 and LF59 remove what they break).
4. Cross-backend acceptance criteria (the loss histories of PyTorch and JAX agree over the first iterations within a set tolerance), once LF1 gives JAX its synthesis optimiser; this task does not hold up LF58 and LF59.

## Done when

- the reference tests run in the full tier and fail when a synthesis value changes beyond `tests/tolerances.toml`; `tools/version_impact.py` then requires a bump of `SYNTHESIS_REFERENCE`;
- each case of task 1 follows STL's loss within the tolerance of T6.1;
- after LF1, the cross-backend criterion passes on PyTorch and JAX.

## Feature

Synthesis tests: per-backend reference outputs, cross-backend acceptance criteria, synthesis parity with STL; the comparison of the loss with STL's over the first iterations of a 2D synthesis is in step 1 (Part II, §15.2, T6.1)
