## Goal

The four v1.6.0 estimators and `method="auto"` on 1D and tensor-product data (§20.5): spectra of non-periodic data and of data with missing values in every planar geometry.

## Brief

**Question 1. Generalised directly, with LF23, or after LF23 reproduces the spectra of `2D+1D`?** The same question as in LF23.
(a) Directly, one piece of work: one maths entry, one implementation, no reference bump; this entry then changes no published value.
(b) After LF23: a second implementation, a reference bump, and the parity tests of LF23 removed.
**Recommendation:** (a), because no STL behaviour is kept (decisions of 20 November 2026), so (b) ships a spectrum that the next release drops.
**Question 2. Tensor products have one `pbc` per block (M-DATA-TP), v1.6.0 one per field: how do `pbc_mask` and `method="auto"` read it?**
(a) Per block: border masks only along the axes of the non-periodic blocks; `pbc_mask` chosen when any block is non-periodic.
(b) One `pbc` for the data, false when one block is: borders cropped along every axis; simpler, loses the modes of the periodic blocks.
**Recommendation:** (a), because it keeps every mode along the periodic blocks and reads the data as they describe themselves.
**Question 3. The NaN-aware estimators on tensor products need data with missing values, which raise an error until LF8 (step 2b, OP-41).**
(a) LF10 ships 1D in full, and tensor products with `fft` and `pbc_mask`; `nan_mask` and `pbc_nan_mask` on tensor products follow LF8.
(b) LF10 waits for LF8, and moves to step 2b.
**Recommendation:** (a), because the 1D part and the tensor products without NaN do not depend on LF8.
