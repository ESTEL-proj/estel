## Feature

The backward pass of the HEALPix convolution applied with a transposed table built with the plan, instead of the scatter-add of `index_select`

## Why

measured by T5.7 on CPU (PR #183): the gradient of the HEALPix convolution costs 19 to 20 times its forward pass, through the backward of `index_select` inside `gather_weighted`; it dominates syntheses: at `nside` 128, full sky, J = 6, a forward pass takes 1.0 s and a forward pass with the gradient 6.6 s, against a build of the tables of 25.6 s done once

## Builds on (reserved in 1.0.0)

`gather_weighted` and the HEALPix tables (T5.1, T5.7); the differentiation contract (Part I, §3.2)

## To decide

whether a rule through `custom_jvp` (Part I, §3.2.3 forbids `custom_vjp` in the Python engine and allows `custom_jvp`, which JAX transposes), with its PyTorch counterpart, keeps the second order on PyTorch and JAX; the memory of the transposed tables; whether the Rust engine (LF3, with its hand-written gradients) makes it unnecessary

## Details

related to LF1 (the memory of `gather_weighted` on JAX), LF36 (level-wise gradients) and LF62 (the benchmark). Suggested step: 2a.5 (the optimisation session) or 2b
