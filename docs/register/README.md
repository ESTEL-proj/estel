# Register of later features

Generated by `tools/register.py`; do not edit; run `make register`. `make check` fails when a card is invalid or this view is out of date. In a terminal, `python tools/register.py` prints a view, filtered by `--step`, `--status` or `--human`.

The register gathers everything still to implement after step 1: every development planned, and every feature, optimisation or piece of work deferred during the reviews or by an open point of the maths tab, so that none is forgotten. Until 20 November 2026 it was the table of Part III, §16. Each entry is now a card, `docs/register/<ID>.md`, and this view is made from the front matter of the cards. The decisions on the register and on the later steps are logged in its decision log, [decisions.md](decisions.md).

## How an entry lives

A remark made while working on the mathematics opens an open point of the maths tab (OP). An open point is decided and applied at once, closed as not relevant, or sent here as a card. Any discussion may add open points or cards. What an entry still has to decide is in its section "To decide", summed up by its field `decide`, and is settled by the time the entry is implemented. When a step opens (Part III, §19.13), each of its entries becomes one GitHub issue, given by its field `issue` and broken into tasks whose progress is followed in comments on the issue, and the step is broken into phases, as step 1 is (Part II, §15). When an entry is done, its issue is closed and its status becomes `done`: the card stays, in the section "Done" of this view.

One card per entry, named after its identifier: LFn ("later feature") and Wn (deferred work that is not a feature); a new entry takes the next free number. An entry that is abandoned, or that becomes a task of step 1 (Part II, §15.2), loses its card and leaves a row in a decision log: that of the specification (Part IV, §22) or that of the register ([decisions.md](decisions.md)).

## A card

A front matter in TOML between two lines `+++`, then the text of the entry: one section per column of the former table, copied verbatim on 20 November 2026 (Feature, Why, Builds on (reserved in 1.0.0), To decide, Details, Source), and the section "Step before 20 November 2026", its column "Step" as it was. In that text, a section number without a part (§20.11) is one of Part III, and "the maths tab" is `docs/maths.md`. The section "Source" says where the entry comes from: a review point (Part IV, §22) or an open point of the maths tab (OP). The fields of the front matter:

- **`id`**: the identifier, the name of the file.
- **`title`**: at most 8 words, faithful to the section "Feature".
- **`category`**: *Structure*: backends and engines, nothing the user sees, everything the code is built on. *New feature*: what users will be able to compute or do that they cannot today. *Optimisation*: the same results, faster, larger, lighter or on more configurations. *Mathematical choice*: a decision that changes what existing statistics compute; its definition is in `docs/maths_step2.md` where one is given. *Work that is not a feature*: the deferred work (Wn).
- **`step`**: `queue` (not placed yet), `2a`, `2b` or `3`, placed by the criterion of [decisions.md](decisions.md) (Part III, §19.13).
- **`phase`**: for step 2a only, one of the phases below; empty otherwise.
- **`status`**: one of the statuses below.
- **`decide`**: what the entry still has to settle, by the time it is implemented, in short phrases: its section "To decide", and the questions of its section "Brief".
- **`depends`**: the entries it must wait for, named in its column "Step" ("with LF62"); an entry that completes it later ("completed by LF22") is not one.
- **`changes_values`**: whether the reference values change, and the version impact where it is known.
- **`issue`**: the number of its GitHub issue; 0 until it has one.

## Statuses

- **`decision`**: a human must decide before an agent can be tasked: the entry has something to decide; or it changes values and `docs/maths_step2.md` gives no `M-` definition for it; or its scope is a human choice ("a proposal, to examine", "only on a specific need", "if validated", a research question).
- **`study`**: the entry is to be measured, tried or studied, and the result informs a decision; an agent can be tasked with the study.
- **`ready`**: none of the above; an agent can be tasked.
- **`in-progress`**: the work has started, under its issue.
- **`done`**: done; its issue is closed.

Below, each step is split into "Needs a human" (`decision`) and "Can be treated" (`study`, `ready`, `in-progress`). A maintainer reviews the statuses.

- **2a.1**: means and borders.
- **2a.2**: the coefficient set and the reductions.
- **2a.3**: spectra and masks.
- **2a.4**: syntheses.
- **2a.5**: the end of step 2a: the benchmark baseline and the optimisation session.
- **2a.X**: the additive lane: the entries that do not hold up the end of step 2a, at any time.

2a.0 → 2a.1 → 2a.2 → 2a.5 is the critical path; 2a.3 and 2a.4 run in parallel from 2a.0; 2a.X runs at any time.

## The queue

Something found during step 1 that belongs to a later step (2a, 2b or 3) becomes a new card with `step = "queue"` and `status = "decision"` (placing it is a human decision), the next free LF number, and optionally a suggested step in its body. Never change the step, phase, status or content of an existing card unless your task names that card (AGENTS.md, golden rule 16).
