## Feature

A treatment of the edge of a partial HEALPix sky, where the stencil of the convolution or the decimation reaches pixels that are not in `cell_ids`: those pixels left out of the means, weighted, or marked, as the borders of non-periodic planar data are cropped

## Why

in 1.0.0, as in STL, nothing is cropped on a partial sky: the convolution and the decimation renormalise their weights over the available pixels, a stencil point without any available pixel reads the pixel itself, and every pixel enters the means. On a disc of 154 pixels at `nside` 16, 121 pixels have a truncated stencil, and their values differ from the full-sky ones by a relative rms of 0.48 (maths OP-50)

## Builds on (reserved in 1.0.0)

the static masks and reweighting maps of HEALPix data (maths M-MASK-HPX); the tables of the port (Part II, §9.3)

## To decide

how the truncated pixels are treated (left out of the means, weighted, or marked); whether the treatment is the default, which changes values, or an option

## Details

maths §4.1 (partial sky), §4.3, §4.4, OP-50; Part IV, §22, row of 24 November 2026, "The edge of a partial sky". The planar counterpart is LF57. Suggested step: 2a

## Source

decision of the maintainer Erwan Allys on the breakdown of P5, 24 November 2026: the edge of a partial sky is reproduced as in STL in 1.0.0, and handled later
