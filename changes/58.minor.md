Add the planar DataClasses (task T2.1, Part II §12.4): `estel.DataClass`, the immutable mother class, and `estel.Data1D` and `estel.Data2D`. A DataClass holds one backend array, promoted to `(Nb, Nc, *spatial)` (maths.md M-DATA-2D, M-DATA-1D, OP-29), an explicit boolean `pbc` (OP-4) and a static mask of missing values, read from the NaN of a single map or passed as `mask=` (OP-26); `with_array`, `copy` and batch and channel slicing return new objects. The static masks and reweighting maps of the scattering layers (M-DOWN-MASK, M-MASK) are built once in NumPy by `estel.data._masks`, and equal STL's on two new parity fixtures, `masks_2d` (STL `master`) and `masks_1d` (STL `2D+1D`). No reference value changes.
