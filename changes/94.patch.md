Benchmark of step 1 (T2.11): `make bench` measures three 2D cases on CPU, in float32, outside CI (`bench/cases.toml`: 512 × 512 periodic, 512 × 512 non-periodic with 10 % of missing pixels, 256 × 256 with two channels and their cross terms): the time of the forward pass, the time of the forward pass with the gradient of a loss, and the peak resident memory of a process that runs one case, with its baseline before the operator is built. STL and ESTEL are measured in the parity container, where `bench/Dockerfile` installs ESTEL beside STL, and ESTEL natively too. Every ESTEL run is compared with STL's statistics on the same inputs, with the float32 parity tolerances of Part II, §14.3, and the benchmark fails otherwise. Results go to `bench/results/<date>-<machine>.json` with a description of the machine; the first results are recorded. A smoke test runs the harness on a tiny case in the fast tier. No statistic, public name, reference value or tolerance changes; no optimisation. `AGENTS.md` and its mirror describe `make bench` as it now is (maintainer's edit).
