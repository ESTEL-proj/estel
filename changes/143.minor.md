`STOperatorTensorProd`: the scattering operator of tensor products of 1D and 2D blocks (maths.md §3), with STL's loop and cache of prefixes, the second layer as Gram products, the low-pass filter of each block and the centred box term (`lowpass="ST"` or `None`), its statistics in the compact layout of tensor products; `estel.functional.scattering` takes its plan and filters. Planar statistics are unchanged.
