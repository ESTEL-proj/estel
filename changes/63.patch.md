Add the `ScatteringPlan` (task T2.3, Part II §12.6), the static description of a scattering transform built once in NumPy, in the private module `estel.scattering._plan`: the index sets of S1 to S4 in the order of STL's loops, the levels 0 … J−1 and their shapes, the low-pass filter (in 1.0.0 the cascade of the smoothing kernels, at level J), the masks and reweighting maps with the maps of each downsampling step, and the weights and denominator of every mean (maths.md M-BORDER, M-MASK, M-MEAN). Borders follow M-BORDER ("fully flexible"); a border too large for the grid is reduced as in STL, with one warning per plan (OP-13); a mean's invalid pixels are the union of its operands' masks (M-MASK, new OP-35: STL takes the layer-2 mask alone, and its S3 is NaN where that mask misses the layer-1 one); a mean with no pixel left is NaN, as in STL, decided statically: the plan lists such means (`empty_scales`, `empty_s3_pairs`, `empty_s4_triples`), gives them no weights, warns once naming them, and raises only when a whole family is empty (OP-3, revised in maths.md M-MEAN); sizes not divisible by 2^(J−1) are rejected (OP-8). The plan is immutable and hashable. `DataClass` gains the geometry hook `_crop_weights(level, border)`, implemented for `Data1D` and `Data2D`. Borders, denominators and averaged pixels equal STL's on two new parity fixtures, `plan_2d` (STL `master`) and `plan_1d` (STL `2D+1D`), and the empty means are exactly where STL's statistics are NaN on two more, `empty_means_2d` and `empty_means_1d`. No public name or reference value changes.
