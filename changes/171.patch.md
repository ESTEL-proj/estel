The parity cases of HEALPix at STL `HealpixUpdate` (T5.3, #164), with their fixtures: healpix-analyse's convolution and decimation as dense matrices at `nside` 4 and as responses to seeded maps at `nside` 8 and 16, full sky and a disc (`operators_hpx`); the statistics with each normalisation and reduction, on the full sky, two channels, a disc and NaN masks, with the masks and reweighting maps of M-MASK-HPX (`statistics_hpx`); the first 20 iterations of a full-sky synthesis (`synthesis_hpx`). STL runs on the sphere, without its geometry cache, with its kernel kept in float64 (`tools/stl_parity/cases/_healpix.py`). healpy 1.20.1 joins the dev dependencies, test-only. Existing fixtures are unchanged; no public name or reference value changes.
