HEALPix, internal optimisations (T5.7, #168), no public name and no value beyond the parity tolerance: the stencils and the support test of the masks are built by batches of output pixels; the stencils are gathered by batches of rows inside `gather_weighted` (an internal `batch_rows` argument of the L0 primitive); a masked operator builds the tables of each level once; and the kernel is folded into the stencil tables. At `nside` 128 the build of an operator falls from 58 to 26 s, its peak memory from 11.9 to 5.6 GiB, the forward pass from 1.29 to 1.00 s. The benchmark gains the case `healpix-masked-64`, and times the build of the operator apart.
