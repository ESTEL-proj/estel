The wavelet operators and the modules of `wavelets/` follow the tree of geometries and the rule of names (task T7.6, #197). No value changes.

- `WaveletOperator`, renamed `WaveletOperatorSingleDomain` by script, comes back as the abstract root of `WaveletOperatorSingleDomain` and `WaveletOperatorTensorProd` (formerly `TensorProdWaveletOperator`), and declares what generic code calls on any wavelet operator (Part I, §2.4.1, rule 6). Building it raises.
- The modules of one geometry are named after it: `_banks_cartesian.py`, `_banks_healpix.py`, `_tables_healpix.py`, `_pixels_healpix.py`, `_operator_healpix.py`, `_operator_tensor_prod.py`; `_operator.py` stays bare. The import contracts forbid the same modules, renamed.
- The HEALPix names end with `_healpix` (`stencils_healpix`, `decimation_healpix`, `kernel_weights_healpix`, `StencilsHealpix`, `DecimationHealpix`, …), and the names that serve Cartesian data only, outside the Cartesian modules, with `_cartesian` (`smoothing_kernel_cartesian`, `KERNEL_SIZE_CARTESIAN`, `border_width_cartesian`, …).
