The bricks of the power spectrum follow the tree of geometries and the rule of names (task T7.9, #200). No value changes, and the files of `estel.io` are unchanged.

- The bricks that compute on 2D grids only end with `2D`: `SpectrumEstimatorPlan2D`, `SpectrumEstimatorFilters2D`, `SpectrumBins2D`, `build_spectrum_plan_2d`, `log_gaussian_bins_2d`, the four estimators `estimate_fft_2d`, `estimate_pbc_mask_2d`, `estimate_nan_mask_2d`, `estimate_pbc_nan_mask_2d`, and their kin.
- The plan of the spectrum lives in `spectra/_estimators.py` with the other bricks of the spectrum, moved from `scattering/_plan.py`; `estel.spectra` imports nothing from `estel.scattering`.
- What holds nothing of 2D stays bare: the names of `spectrum_method`, `choose_estimator`, `resolve_compute_ps`, the layout and the normalisation of the family `"PS"`, and `bessel_j0`.
