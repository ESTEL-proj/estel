Tensor products of planar geometries, the data and the dispatch along each block's axes (task T3.1, #22; Part II, §12.4; maths.md M-DATA-TP, M-W1-TP). New public class `estel.DataTensorProd(array, blocks, pbc)`, with the properties `blocks`, `pbc` (one boolean per block) and `ndim`: one array whose spatial axes are those of its blocks, in block order, any list of `Data2D` and `Data1D` blocks, a single block included (OP-82); a pytree whose only leaf is the array, the blocks static. Missing values raise at construction and name LF8 (OP-41). Its hooks `_conv`, `_downsample` and `_crop_weights` act on one block, named by `block=`, and dispatch to the geometry of that block along its own axes, without moving any axis: the planar geometries gain the same operations along given axes, which their own hooks now call, with no change to their values. The internal wavelet operator of a tensor product (`estel.wavelets._tensor_prod`) is composed from the plans and arrays of its blocks, with multi-index levels: the kernels of the 2D operator and of the 1D operator with L = 2, the defaults of M-DEF-TP, J and L validated at construction as lists of one entry per block without modifying the caller's lists (OP-83), and every size of block a divisible by 2^(J_a - 1) (OP-8). `STOperator` refuses a `DataTensorProd` with a message that names `STOperatorTensorProd` (T3.2). No statistic, tolerance or reference value changes.
