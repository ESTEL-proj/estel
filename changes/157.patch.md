The 2D power spectrum as a family `"PS"` of `STStatistics`, before its operator (task T4.4, #151; maths.md §1.9, M-CHAN, M-NORM, M-FLAT; Part III, §19.4 to §19.6). The internal plan of `STOperator` can describe the spectrum (n_b, J_PS, J_min, its channel pairs, those of `cross_matrix`), with clear errors on data that are not 2D, an odd size (OP-20) and J_min outside 0 … J_PS − 2 (OP-93); `STOperator` does not ask for it until T4.1. PS is the last segment of the compact layout, at level 0, one chunk per channel pair c₁ ≤ c₂ holding its bins, one float per entry; its axes are `(c1, c2, bin)`. The normalisation divides it by σᴾˢ_{c₁}(b) σᴾˢ_{c₂}(b), with σᴾˢ held in the reference beside σ, and a reference from another operator must share n_bins, J and Jmin; `to_vector` puts it last, its real part only; `select(channel=(a, b))` returns it with the scattering statistics of that pair; the reductions leave it unchanged. `estel.io` writes it: `SCHEMA_VERSION` 6, files of version 5 refused. Placed in the layout from the fixture `statistics_ps_2d`, the positions, the normalised statistics, their references and the four vectors equal STL's. No public name and no reference value changes; planar statistics are unchanged, float for float.
