The compact coefficient layout (task T2.15, #93; Part III, §19.2, §19.4 to §19.9). `STStatistics` now stores its statistics in one real buffer `(Nb, n_floats)`, its only array leaf with those of the reference and the standardisation: every coefficient STL computes and only those, real entries as one float (mean, var, the auto S1 and S2, the diagonal of S4), complex entries as (re, im), STL's conjugate duplicates kept (option a of §19.8). The internal `Layout` and `Segment` (`estel/statistics/_layout.py`) describe it: one segment per family and level, with its named index table. The loop takes one static gather from its divided sums to the buffer (`scattering._plan.loop_sums`, `_layout.loop_gather`, in `STFilters.gather` instead of the three coefficient indices), and writes no full-grid tensor; `full_grid` builds STL's view on demand, with `scatter_to_grid`, and `scattering()` calls none (a test asserts it). The normalisation, `select` and the reductions `iso`, `angular_fourier` and `scale_cosine` are static linear maps on the buffer, built in NumPy from the layout and applied with `take` and `gather_weighted`; `angular_fourier` is applied one orientation axis at a time, and the tables of a layout have the size of its stored entries, not of the full grid. The normalisation reference stores its scales compactly and builds STL's `S2_ref_sqrt_chan_diag` on demand. `estel.io` writes the buffer and the layout: `SCHEMA_VERSION` 3, files of version 2 refused. `full_grid`, `select`, `to_vector`, the complex flattening of the syntheses, `reduce`, `save` and `load` keep their signatures and values, within the parity tolerance; no public name changes, no reference value changes.
