Task T2.16 (#98), the consistency of the estimators (Part I, §5.1), without a change to `src/`. A new family of tests in the full tier, `tests/consistency/` (PyTorch, float64, 24 s of CPU time): the statistics of one stationary, non-Gaussian process drawn by FFT, estimated on a periodic field, on the same field read as non-periodic (`pbc=False`) and on the same field with a mask of 10.1 %, agree within 5.5 standard errors measured over 64 seeded draws (4, corrected for the 12 356 comparisons), for every coefficient whose mean keeps a quarter of the pixels of its level, at 128 × 128 and 256 × 256. The estimates are paired with the periodic one, field by field; a window cut from a larger field, which has the same expectation as the non-periodic estimate and ten times its standard error, was measured and not kept. No systematic gap was found at these sizes. `docs/maths.md` gains OP-60, at the maintainer's request: with missing data STL's gradient is NaN at every valid pixel because its default `replace_nan_value=NaN` puts NaN values under a modulus and products that the masked mean then ignores (0 × NaN in the backward pass); ESTEL keeps a finite gradient. No public name or reference value changes.
