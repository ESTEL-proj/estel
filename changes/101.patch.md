A new family of tests in the full tier, `tests/consistency/` (PyTorch, float64, 48 s of CPU time): the statistics of one stationary, non-Gaussian process drawn by FFT, estimated on a periodic field, on a non-periodic window cut from a field four times larger and with a mask of 10.1 %, agree within 5.5 standard errors measured over seeded draws (4, corrected for the 20 200 comparisons), for every coefficient whose mean keeps a quarter of the pixels of its level, at 128 × 128 (256 draws) and 256 × 256 (64 draws); a fourth, paired estimate, the periodic field read as non-periodic, tests the borders more closely. No systematic gap was found. `docs/maths.md` gains OP-60, at the maintainer's request: with missing data STL's gradient is NaN at every valid pixel because its default `replace_nan_value=NaN` puts NaN values under a modulus and products that the masked mean then ignores (0 × NaN in the backward pass); ESTEL keeps a finite gradient.
