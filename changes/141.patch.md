The compact layout stored by chunks (task T3.7, #139; Part III, §19.4 to §19.6). The internal `Layout` keeps, for each family, one row per channel pair and scale combination (`FamilyGrid.chunks`), each chunk holding the dense array of its orientations, with the kind of each chunk saying which of its entries take one float; a `Segment` is a range of these chunks, with the shape of its orientations. No table per coefficient is kept: the coordinates of an entry and `FamilyGrid.present` are computed on demand. The buffer is unchanged, float for float. The gather from the sums of the loop, the normalisation, `iso`, `angular_fourier`, `scale_cosine`, `select`, `to_vector`, the complex flattening and `full_grid` act chunk by chunk, with tables of one row per chunk and small matrices on the orientations; the indices the size of the coefficients they read are built inside the computation, never kept or compiled as constants; on 2D data with J = 5 and L = 4 the static arrays of the layout and of its normalisation fall from about 140,000 elements to about 2,100, for 2,522 coefficients. The cached maps of the reductions and of the normalisation, and the flattening of `to_vector`, are compiled once where the backend compiles (JAX), the tables of the chunks as arguments. `estel.io` writes the layout by chunks: `SCHEMA_VERSION` 4, files of version 3 refused. No public name and no reference value changes; `angular_fourier` and `scale_cosine` differ from `main` by rounding only.
