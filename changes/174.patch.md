The HEALPix geometry tables, ported from STL's `TorchGeometry` into NumPy (T5.1, #26): the wavelet kernel (`healpix_kernel_weights`), the interpolation tables of the rotated stencils (`healpix_stencils`) and the decimation tables (`healpix_decimation`), in private modules of `estel.wavelets`, not yet used by an operator (T5.5). The rows of these functions in Part II, §12.5 take their decided signatures. No public name or reference value changes.
