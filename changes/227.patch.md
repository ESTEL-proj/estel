P5-02 of the final review: the HEALPix stencil tables are built faster. `_bind_support` looks up the neighbours of each stencil point once instead of twice, by their offset when the pixels form one range (the full sky), so `searchsorted`, 38 to 44 % of the build of a HEALPix operator, is gone from the full sky and halved elsewhere: on `healpix-masked-64` the build takes ×0.58, on a disc at `nside` 64 ×0.82. The tables are equal bit for bit. Internal only: no public name or reference value changes.
