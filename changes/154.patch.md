The parity cases of the 2D power spectrum at STL `master` (T4.3, #150), with their fixtures: the spectrum operator with its four estimators, its automatic choice, auto and cross spectra, rectangular images and the default bins (`spectrum_2d`); the statistics with the spectrum, its reference and the flat vectors (`statistics_ps_2d`); the first 20 iterations of a periodic synthesis with the spectrum in the loss (`synthesis_ps_2d`). Existing fixtures are unchanged; no public name or reference value changes.
