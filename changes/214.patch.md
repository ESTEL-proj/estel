The scattering plans, filters and loops follow the tree of geometries and the rule of names (task T7.7, #198). No value changes.

- The plans and filters of each geometry are named after it: `STPlanSingleDomain`, `STPlanTensorProd`, `STFiltersSingleDomain`, `STFiltersTensorProd` (formerly `PlanarPlan`, `TensorProdPlan`, `PlanarFilters`, `TensorProdFilters`), under the public roots `STPlan` and `STFilters`, which the annotations that mean any plan or any filters now name.
- The loops are in modules of their own: `scattering/_loop_single_domain.py` (`values_single_domain`) and `scattering/_loop_tensor_prod.py` (`values_tensor_prod`, formerly `_loop_tp.py`); `scattering/_loop.py` keeps, bare, the hook `_global_sum` and what both loops share.
- Both loops call `_global_sum` through its module at each call: a replaced hook now reaches the loop of tensor products too, which imported it by name. Its reserved test covers both loops.
