Task T2.12 (#90), the Gram form of the second layer (maths_step2 M-GRAM, LF31). The second-layer maps of a level come from one convolution, with the band orientation as their leading axis: the hook `_conv` and `WaveletOperator.apply` now return the orientation axis of the convolution right after the batch axis, `(Nb, L, Nc, *spatial)`, for the first and the second layer alike; each map is set to zero outside its own window, the S4 sums of the level are one matrix product A A^H and the S3 sums one product with the band map, by blocks of 16,384 pixels added in float64 in float32 runs, and each mean is divided by the number of pixels of its pair's window, so that STL's windows are reproduced exactly, masks and borders included. The weights of the means are stored once per distinct level, border and mask. `make bench` now measures the ESTEL of `origin/main` before the working tree, natively, and fails unless the statistics agree within the float32 parity tolerances and the gradient of the loss within 1e-4. Specification edits at the maintainer's request (`docs/transfer/part1.md`, `part2.md`, `part4.md`, `docs/maths_step2.md`): the optimisations T2.12 to T2.15 come before the planar syntheses, and compare their gradients with those of `main` on the benchmark's cases. Every value stays within the parity tolerance: no public name or reference value changes.
