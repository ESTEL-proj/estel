Task T2.16 (#98), the consistency of the estimators (Part I, §5.1), without a change to `src/`. A new family of tests in the full tier, `tests/consistency/` (PyTorch, float64, 17 s of CPU time on one thread): the statistics of one stationary, non-Gaussian process drawn by FFT, estimated on a periodic field, on the same field read as non-periodic (`pbc=False`) and on the same field with a mask of 10.1 %, agree within 5.5 standard errors of their paired difference (4, corrected for the number of comparisons) over 64 seeded draws, for every coefficient whose mean keeps a quarter of the pixels of its level, at 128 × 128 and 256 × 256. A positive control at 64 × 64 (512 draws) shows that the test sees a gap: there the border of the coarsest level is reduced (OP-13) and the non-periodic S2 is 18.8 to 24.7 % below the periodic one, S1 10.2 to 13.7 %, in STL as in ESTEL; the test asserts that this gap is seen and stays within 1.5 times its measured size. `docs/maths.md` gains two open points, at the maintainer's request: OP-60, with missing data STL's gradient is NaN at every valid pixel because its default `replace_nan_value=NaN` puts NaN values under a modulus and products that the masked mean then ignores (0 × NaN in the backward pass), and ESTEL keeps a finite gradient; OP-61, the gap of the positive control, with a proposal for step 2. Part I, §5.1, Part II, §15.2 and Part IV describe the paired design. No public name or reference value changes.
