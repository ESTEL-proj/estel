The step from the Gram products of the second layer to the chunks of the compact buffer is shared by the two scattering loops (task P3-02 of the final review; Part III, §19.3, §19.9). In `scattering._loop`, `gram_chunk_sums` reads the S3 and S4 chunks of a scale j3 with tables that grow with the chunks. `_divide_level` divides them chunk by chunk, `first_layer_floats` orders S1 and S2, and `compact_buffer` assembles the buffer and packs S4. The single-domain loop no longer slices the products sum by sum. Its gather in `statistics._layout` (`loop_sums_single_domain`, `loop_gather_single_domain`, `gather_loop_single_domain`) gives way to `gather_single_domain`, the counterpart of `gather_tensor_prod`. Every output stays bitwise equal (`make prove`, verdict 0). On PyTorch with one thread, the forward pass with the gradient takes 0.83 of its time on `planar-cross` and 0.97 on `planar-periodic`. On JAX at 512², J = 7, the compile time of the forward and of the gradient falls to 0.28 and 0.26 of its value at three channels, and to 0.89 and 0.80 at one. No public name, statistic or reference value changes.
