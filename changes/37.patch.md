Add the PyTorch backend of the L0 primitives (T1.2): `estel.backend.torch._primitives.TorchBackend` implements every primitive of `estel.backend._protocol` (Part II, §12.3), out of place and differentiable to second order in reverse and forward mode; `lbfgs` drives `torch.optim.LBFGS(line_search_fn="strong_wolfe")` one iteration at a time, with the same iterates as a single `step`, per-iteration losses and a resumable state. The conformance suite `tests/backend/test_conformance.py` checks values, VJPs, JVPs, gradients and Hessian-vector products of every primitive against NumPy float64, in float32 and float64. Internal only: no public name changes.
