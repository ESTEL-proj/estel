The planar and tensor-product objects related through their hierarchy (T3.8, #144). `Data.ndim` is now a read-only property of the mother class instead of a class constant: the entry `estel.Data.ndim` of `api/manifest.json` changes from kind `constant` to `property`, authorised by the maintainer on #144. No value changes, on classes or instances: `Data1D.ndim` and `Data2D.ndim` stay class constants (1 and 2), `DataTensorProd.ndim` stays the property that counts the axes of its blocks, and the mother class, never instantiated, has no value. `Data.pbc` is annotated `bool | tuple[bool, ...]`, one boolean per block for a tensor product. Internally, the behaviour that depends on the geometry goes through hooks of `STPlan` and `STFilters` instead of `isinstance` branches, and internal functions that had one name for two things are renamed or merged. No statistic changes.
