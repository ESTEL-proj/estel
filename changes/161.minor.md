`SpectrumOperator`: the 2D power spectrum of STL v1.6.0 (maths.md §1.9) as `STStatistics` holding the family `"PS"` alone, with its four estimators and the automatic choice, its bins, borders and Bessel function J₀ in NumPy, normalised as `STOperator` (`norm`, `ref_batch_mean`), and its pure function `estel.functional.spectrum`. `STOperator` computes the spectrum by default for 2D data (`compute_PS`), with `n_bins`, `Jmin` and `spectrum_method`; `STStatistics.bin_centers` gives the centres of the bins in cycles per pixel. Files: schema version 7. With `compute_PS=False`, no value changes.
