The normalisations `store_ref` and `load_ref` follow STL (maths.md M-NORM): the operator keeps the `store_ref` reference, internal, and replaces it with a `UserWarning`; `load_ref` takes an `STStatistics` computed with `store_ref`; gradients flow through a `store_ref` reference and not through a `load_ref` one (LF21, closed); `norm_batch_mean` is False by default, as in STL's code (maths.md M-NORM and M-DEF corrected). `STStatistics` has `full_grid(family)`, one `select(family=None, **index)` on named axes, `to_vector(mask_st=None)` as STL's `to_flatten(flatten_complex=True)` (OP-37, new: the imaginary parts of the cross-channel S1, S2 and PS are dropped), the reductions `iso()`, `angular_ft(harmonics)` and `scale_ft(harmonics, dj)` (M-RED-ISO, M-RED-ANG, M-RED-SCALE), and `save(path)` / `STStatistics.load(path)` through the versioned schema of the new `estel.io`; it is a pytree on both backends. Every absent coefficient (structural, empty means OP-3, OP-30, missing scales of `scale_ft`) is known statically, so `to_vector` and the reductions never look for NaN and no NaN reaches the gradients. The loop now sums, passes each level's stacked sums to the internal MPI hook `_global_sum` (the identity in 1.0.0), then divides. `ScatteringPlan` and `ScatteringFilters` (renamed from `ScatteringArrays`) are opaque: names only. New parity fixtures `statistics_2d` (STL `master`) and `statistics_1d` (STL `2D+1D`): `store_ref` and `load_ref` with `norm_batch_mean` False and True, masks, cross channels, OP-30, empty means, every reduction and the flat vectors; all equal STL's in float64 and float32.
