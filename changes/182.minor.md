`synthesize_from_maps` and `synthesize_from_stats` take full-sky HEALPix fields (task T5.8, issue #169; maths.md section 5): a `DataHealpix` target without `cell_ids` and without missing values, or the statistics of one, synthesised without the pre-filter and without the spectrum (OP-77), with the default J of M-DEF-HPX. A `DataHealpix` target keeps its channel axis in the result (OP-75). Partial and masked skies (LF47), `compute_PS=True` (LF24) and `running_shape` (OP-101) raise on HEALPix data with an error that says what to do. The pre-filter of 2D syntheses is now the geometry hook `Data._nyquist_filter`, with its values unchanged. 2D syntheses are unchanged.
