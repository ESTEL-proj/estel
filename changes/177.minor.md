HEALPix data: `DataHealpix(array, mask=None, *, depth=None, cell_ids=None, partition=None)` (task T5.5, issue #166; maths.md M-DATA-HPX). NESTED ordering, one pixel axis, the full sky or the partial sky of `cell_ids`, strictly increasing (OP-90) and refused when they cover the whole sphere at the given `depth` (omit them for a full sky); `depth` read from the number of pixels on a full sky and required with `cell_ids` (OP-91); `pbc` a read-only property, True on a full sky and False with `cell_ids`; the mask read from the NaN of a single map or given (OP-26); `partition` reserved, `None` only (LF34). New public names: `DataHealpix`, its `depth`, `cell_ids`, `pbc` and `ndim`. Its internal wavelet operator applies the tables of T5.1 with `gather_weighted` and `segment_sum`, with the masks and reweighting maps of M-MASK-HPX, and equals the oracle and STL's fixtures (on the discs, off the ill-conditioned rows of OP-110). The wavelet plan and filters gain internal hooks (`_build_filters`, `_conv_tables`, `_down_tables`, `_any_array`), the internal `wavelet` argument defaults to `None`, the wavelet of the geometry (`"bump_steerable"` for 1D and 2D data, `"healpix"` for `DataHealpix`), `Data._from_parts` carries the static metadata of the geometry (`Data._meta`), and one mask builder serves every geometry; planar values do not change.
