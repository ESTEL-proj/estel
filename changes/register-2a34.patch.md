Register, decisions of 25 November 2026 on phases 2a.3 and 2a.4. For LF58: the synthesis loss is not divided, from statistics as from maps, a choice documented in the docstrings of `synthesize_from_maps` and `synthesize_from_stats` and in the documentation, and the logs of a synthesis show, beside the loss, an intensive loss, the loss divided by the number of real terms it sums, whose typical ranges the documentation gives, measured on the synthesis tests of W2; one concept for `mean_field`, the pooled standardisation of synthesis from maps everywhere, through a batch standardisation of the statistics API, so that synthesis from maps becomes synthesis from the statistics of the pre-filtered maps; the result follows the layout of the target, with `nbatch` in place of the batch axis, the statistics recording the layout of their input. For LF59: the start is filtered, then the batch of starts is centred and scaled together to mean 0 and variance 1; a new section, "The Nyquist disk", restricts the degrees of freedom of the model to the Nyquist disk: the target's statistics are pre-filtered (a pre-filter option of the statistics API, with a warning for statistics computed without it), the loss is computed on the projection of the running field to the disk, and the pre-filter may also be a kernel filtering option. For LF64: the Nyquist pre-filter cuts at the exact physical frequency, (k₁/N₁)² + (k₂/N₂)² ≤ 1/4, an ellipse in mode numbers, square fields unchanged, the same physical frequency as the binning of LF49, on which LF64 now depends. For LF49: the 2D spectrum is binned at the exact physical frequency of each mode, with no radial index and no rounding, which also removes the rounding bias of square images; its registered `changes_values` follows; its question on the synthesis pre-filter is settled by LF64. A new entry, LF69 (step 3): statistics beyond the Nyquist disk. The cards stay `decision` until their `M-` entries exist. Documentation only: no change to `src/`, to the specification, to any statistic or reference value.
