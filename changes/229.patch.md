The band estimators of the 2D power spectrum (`pbc_mask`, `nan_mask`, `pbc_nan_mask`) take the Gram form: one matrix product per batch element and bin over the channels, instead of two copies of the band maps per channel pair (final review of step 1, finding P4-01). The memory of the computation of a non-periodic 512 × 512 field with the spectrum is 6 % lower with one channel and 15 % lower with three, the time unchanged. The spectrum changes by rounding only (at most 2.4e-7 relative in float32 and 2.2e-16 in float64 on the cases of `make prove`). No public name or reference value changes.
