Differentiation tests of the scattering statistics, to second order (task T2.7): `tests/differentiation/` checks the guarantees of Part I, §3.2.2 for `estel.functional.scattering`, and so for `STOperator.apply`, in float64 against finite differences. Every coefficient that exists is differentiated, real and imaginary parts, on 1D and 2D data, with `pbc` on and off, a mask, two channels with their cross terms, `standardize`, a batch and the three normalisations. On PyTorch: `torch.autograd.gradcheck` (reverse and forward mode) and `gradgradcheck`; on JAX: `jax.test_util.check_grads(order=2, modes=("fwd", "rev"))`; on both, the `jvp` and `hvp` primitives, and the normalisation reference, which is differentiated with `norm="self"` and a constant with `norm="ref"` (M-NORM). One small argmin problem built on the statistics checks implicit differentiation end to end (Part I, §3.2.1), on PyTorch. The fast tier gets one test, the `gradcheck` of the smallest case (8 pixels, J = 2); the rest is marked slow and runs in the full tier. No defect was found: no code of `src/`, statistic, public name or reference value changes.
