The power spectrum is computed by `STOperator` only, with its own options `J_PS` and `Jmin_PS`. `J_PS` defaults to ⌊log₂ N_max⌋ − 1 whatever the scattering J. STL passes its J to its spectrum when J is given; pass `J_PS=J` for STL's spectrum (maths.md OP-120). `Jmin` of `STOperator` is renamed `Jmin_PS`. The syntheses take `J_PS` in `st_kwargs`. `SpectrumOperator` and `estel.functional.spectrum` are removed: compute the spectrum with `STOperator(data, J, compute_PS=True, J_PS=…)` (J = 1 takes every size) and `select("PS")`. Files: schema version 9; files of version 8, those of `SpectrumOperator` among them, are refused. With J given and other than the default, the spectrum's values change unless `J_PS=J` is passed. This is a major change: public names are removed, and an argument of `estel.STOperator` is renamed.
