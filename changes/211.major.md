The data classes follow the tree of geometries and the rule of names (task T7.4, #195). No value changes.

- `Data1D` and `Data2D` move from `estel.data._planar` to `estel.data._cartesian`, under a new internal base, `DataCartesian`, which holds their geometry hooks as its own methods. Their `__module__` changes, so pickles of `Data1D` and `Data2D` written by an earlier version no longer load: rebuild the objects from their arrays. Files of `STStatistics.save` (schema 10) are not affected.
- The blocks of a tensor product are the classes that declare the capability of a block: `Data2D` and `Data1D`, as before, with the same error for any other block. `BlockSpec` becomes `BlockSpecTensorProd`.
- The hook `_spectrum_geometry` becomes `_spectrum_grids`; `HealpixConvTables`, `HealpixDownTables` and `HEALPIX_SUPPORT_THRESHOLD` become `ConvTablesHealpix`, `DownTablesHealpix` and `SUPPORT_THRESHOLD_HEALPIX`.
