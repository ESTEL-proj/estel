The statistics and the syntheses follow the tree of geometries and the rule of names (task T7.8, #199). No value changes, and the files of `estel.io` are unchanged.

- The tensor-product names of `statistics/` and `synthesis/` end with their geometry: `grids_tensor_prod`, `auto_scales_tensor_prod`, `layout_tensor_prod`, `gather_tensor_prod`, `BlocksTensorProd`, `blocks_tensor_prod`, `check_reference_tensor_prod` and their kin.
- Every other name of the bare modules of `statistics/` that covers only part of the tree says which part: single-domain (`grids_single_domain`, `layout_single_domain`, `loop_sums_single_domain`, `loop_gather_single_domain`, `gather_loop_single_domain`, `check_reference_single_domain` and their kin) or tensor products (`orientations_at_tensor_prod`, `block_axes_tensor_prod`, `_FULL_GRID_LIMIT_TENSOR_PROD` and their kin).
- The errors of the normalisation reference say "single-domain" where they said "planar", and what to do next. The errors of internal inconsistencies of the scattering loops name public names only, and ask for a bug report.
