Add the scattering loop and an internal `STOperator` (task T2.4, Part II §12.6). The loop keeps STL's structure (for each j3: first layer and S1, S2; for each j2 ≤ j3: second layer and S3; for each j1 ≤ j2: S4; then one downsampling), in functional style: every step returns a new array, the means are collected in lists, and each family is assembled once with `scatter_to_grid` in STL's full-grid shapes and dtypes (Part I §3.1.1; maths.md M-MOM, M-W1, M-S1 to M-S4, M-CHAN). Missing values are weights: the field is zero-filled at its missing pixels before anything else, and every mean reads only the pixels of its plan weights (OP-27); means with no valid pixel are not computed and are NaN in the full grid (OP-3). New internal DataClass functions in `estel.data._moments`: the safe modulus (double `where`), STL's regularised division, `mean_valid`, `cov_valid` (the raw second moment, OP-11, OP-36) and `standardize` (Part II §12.4). The private `estel.scattering._operator.STOperator` accepts `norm="vanilla"` only and returns the full grids; the constructor option `standardize=False` replaces STL's `apply(standardize=...)` (rule A6). `STOperator`, `estel.functional.scattering` and `STStatistics` become public together in T2.5; the public `WaveletOperator` moves to the new task T2.10 (Part II §15.2, edits authorised on #16). Two new parity fixtures, `scattering_2d` (STL `master`) and `scattering_1d` (STL `2D+1D`), cover periodic and open data, masks (OP-26, OP-27), two and three channels with a custom cross matrix (OP-30), standardised data, L = 2 in 1D, and empty means; every family equals STL's in float64. No public name or reference value changes.
