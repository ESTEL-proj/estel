The oracle follows the tree of geometries and the rule of names (task T7.3, #194). No value changes.

- `estel._spec.planar` is split into `estel._spec.common`, which holds what every geometry shares, and `estel._spec.cartesian`, which holds 1D and 2D grids.
- The five Cartesian names that the oracle of tensor products uses end with `_cartesian`: `spec_correlate_cartesian`, `spec_border_cartesian`, `spec_downsample_cartesian`, `_wavelets_cartesian` and `KERNEL_SIZE_CARTESIAN`.
- The `_hpx` and `_tp` names of the oracle become `_healpix` and `_tensor_prod`.
- `spec_tensor_prod` becomes `spec_apply_tensor_prod`, and `spec_spectra` becomes `spec_spectra_2d`.
- In `estel._spec.spectra`, the names that cover 2D grids only end with `_2d`: `spec_spectrum_bins_2d`, `spec_borders_2d`, `spec_spectrum_method_2d`, `_check_shape_2d`, `_radial_index_2d`, `_promote_2d` and `_hard_mask_2d`. Its two constants end with `_2D`: `METHODS_2D` and `ENERGY_FRACTION_2D`.
