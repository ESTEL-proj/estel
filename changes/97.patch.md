Task T2.14 (#92), closed without a change to `src/`. Two optimisations of the memory of the gradient were tried on prototypes and not kept: level-wise gradients (LF36), which lowered no memory after the Gram form and slowed the gradient by 12 to 53 % on CPU, and are measured again on GPU and at larger sizes in T7.1; a first-order gradient on PyTorch (LF46), about 30 % less memory with the gradient but only for a gradient that is not differentiated again, which enters the register of later features. The benchmark gains a fourth case, `planar-open` (512 × 512, non-periodic, without mask), moved from `bench/cases_open.toml`, which is removed, with its first results on `main`. Specification edits at the maintainer's request (`docs/transfer/part1.md` to `part4.md`): the results above, the principle "global optimisations only in 1.0.0", the table of results of the optimisations of step 1 (Part III, §19.14), the rule of comparison of the benchmark (Part I, §6.3), Q30 (TF32 on GPU) and the candidates of the final optimisation session (T7.1). No public name or reference value changes.
