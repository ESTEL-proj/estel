Register and specification, decisions of 25 November 2026 on phases 2a.3 and 2a.4. For LF58: the synthesis loss is not divided, from statistics as from maps, a choice documented in the docstrings of `synthesize_from_maps` and `synthesize_from_stats` and in the documentation, and the logs of a synthesis show, beside the loss, an intensive loss, the loss divided by the number of real terms it sums, whose typical ranges the documentation gives, measured on the synthesis tests of W2; one concept for `mean_field`, the pooled standardisation of synthesis from maps everywhere, through a batch standardisation of the statistics API (a synthesis from statistics computed without it warns and runs on them as they are), so that synthesis from maps becomes synthesis from the statistics of the pre-filtered maps; the result follows the layout of the target, with `nbatch` in place of the batch axis, the statistics recording the layout of their input. For LF59: `var` leaves the synthesis loss, the only statistic of the loss that sees beyond the Nyquist disk, and stays in the statistics; the running field is projected on the disk at every evaluation, so the power beyond it stays zero; the start is the spectral start, a Gaussian field drawn from the target's binned spectrum inside the disk (band-limited white noise with the target's variance inside the disk when the statistics hold no spectrum), the batch of starts scaled together; the Nyquist filter may also be a kernel filtering option; a new section, "The Nyquist disk", gives the reasoning; LF59 depends on LF6. For LF64: the Nyquist pre-filter cuts at the exact physical frequency, (k₁/N₁)² + (k₂/N₂)² ≤ 1/4, an ellipse in mode numbers, square fields unchanged, the same physical frequency as the binning of LF49, on which LF64 now depends. For LF49: the 2D spectrum is binned at the exact physical frequency of each mode, with no radial index and no rounding, which also removes the rounding bias of square images; its registered `changes_values` follows; its question on the synthesis pre-filter is settled by LF64. New entries: LF69 (step 3), statistics beyond the Nyquist disk; LF70 (step 2b), preconditioned syntheses. Part IV, §22, the row Q31: OP-71, OP-72 and OP-75 decided (LF58). The cards stay `decision` until their `M-` entries exist. Documentation only: no change to `src/`, to any statistic or reference value.
