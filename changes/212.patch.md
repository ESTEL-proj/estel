The wavelet plans and filters follow the tree of geometries and the rule of names (task T7.5, #196). No value changes.

- `WaveletPlan` and `WaveletFilters`, renamed `WaveletPlanSingleDomain` and `WaveletFiltersCartesian` by script, come back as the abstract roots of the wavelet plans and filters (Part I, §2.4.1, rule 6). `WaveletPlanSingleDomain` is abstract and holds every field of a single-domain plan, the low-pass fields included (LF6); `WaveletPlanCartesian`, new, holds what only 1D and 2D plans have; `WaveletPlanHealpix` and `WaveletPlanTensorProd` are its siblings. Building an abstract class raises.
- The filters of every geometry are frozen dataclasses under `WaveletFilters`, registered as pytrees with the leaves of the named tuples they replace; the tensor product reads the filters of its blocks only through their hooks.
- `HealpixWaveletPlan`, `HealpixWaveletFilters`, `HealpixPlanTables` and `TensorProdWaveletPlan` become `WaveletPlanHealpix`, `WaveletFiltersHealpix`, `PlanTablesHealpix` and `WaveletPlanTensorProd`; their builders end with `_healpix` and `_tensor_prod`.
