The statistics of tensor products on the compact buffer, before their loop (task T3.6, #137; maths.md M-IDX-TP, M-LP, M-S-TP, M-NORM-TP; Part III, §19.4 to §19.6). An internal `TensorProdPlan` describes the blocks, J, L, the low-pass flags, the channel pairs and the per-block index sets; the layout of tensor products is built by chunks from the Cartesian product of these index sets, never from an array the size of the full grid, one segment per family and multi-index level. A chunk of S1 or S2 at a low-pass scale keeps orientation 0 only along that block: each kind of chunk has its orientation shape, each segment keeps one, and the maps act on one shape at a time. On these statistics, the normalisation (`norm="self"`, `norm="ref"`, `ref_batch_mean`), `to_vector`, `full_grid` (refused over 2 GiB, LF65), `select` by family and channels (LF44), the error of `reduce` (OP-44) and the files work; they equal STL's parity fixtures. `estel.io` writes the statistics of tensor products: `SCHEMA_VERSION` 5, files of version 4 refused. No public name and no reference value changes; planar statistics are unchanged, float for float. The operator comes with T3.2.
