STL parity fixtures of tensor products (T3.4, #127). Three new cases of the `2D+1D` environment:
- `scattering_tp` holds the vanilla statistics of cases A to G of maths §3, of `[1D, 1D]`, of one 2D block and of one 1D block, with `lowpass="ST"` and `None`, plus `standardize` and a cross matrix on case A.
- `statistics_tp` holds the normalisations and the two flat vectors on case C.
- `gradient_tp` holds the gradient of the synthesis loss on case C.

Their S1 to S4 are stored packed (finite entries, shape, packed mask of the finite positions), and `full_grid` in `tests/parity/_compare.py` rebuilds the full grids. A fast-tier test checks the finite positions against M-IDX-TP, M-LP and M-CHAN, and the counts of §3.9. The fixtures take 7.61 MB. No change to `src/`, to an existing fixture or to a reference value.
